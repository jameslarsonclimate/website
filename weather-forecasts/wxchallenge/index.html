Ensembles

ECMWF IFS and AIFS ensembles (51 members each), NCEP GEFS (31; shown and scored but not part of the consensus), all via Open-Meteo, plus two 3 km precipitation ensembles: REFS (NOAA's new operational short-range ensemble: RRFS, RRFS ensemble and HRRR members from the latest two cycles) and HRRRCast (NOAA GSL's experimental AI emulator of the HRRR, 18 members), for the same 06Z–06Z day. Boxes span the middle half of the members (25th–75th percentile), whiskers the full range, the dark bar is the median and the orange diamond the mean; ▼ marks the consensus. The precip axis is square-root scaled so light amounts aren't squeezed by a few wet members. Probabilities are the share of members reaching each amount. Because of how precip is scored, the members' median is the best single precip value to enter.

Chance of precipitation

Chance of at least 0.01" (a trace counts as dry) in each 6-hour block of the 06Z–06Z day and over the whole day. MOS and NBM text guidance (P06) and NBM QMD are calibrated probabilities; the ensembles are the raw share of their members (uncalibrated, and coarse for small ensembles: note the member counts). MOS has 6-hour chances only, so its 24-hour chance is shown as a range: at least the largest block chance, at most the chance if the blocks were independent. None of this goes into the consensus.

Each line holds one source's chance for each 6-hour block (UTC); hover or tap a dot for the value. The table above has every number.
