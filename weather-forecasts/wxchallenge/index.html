Ensembles

ECMWF IFS and AIFS ensembles (51 members each, via Open-Meteo), plus two 3 km precipitation ensembles: REFS (NOAA's new operational short-range ensemble: RRFS, RRFS ensemble and HRRR members from the latest two cycles) and HRRRCast (NOAA GSL's experimental AI emulator of the HRRR, 18 members), for the same 06Z–06Z day. Boxes span the middle half of the members (25th–75th percentile), whiskers the full range, the dark bar is the median and the orange diamond the mean; ▼ marks the consensus. The precip axis is square-root scaled so light amounts aren't squeezed by a few wet members. Probabilities are the share of members reaching each amount. Because of how precip is scored, the members' median is the best single precip value to enter.
