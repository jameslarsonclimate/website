About this page

Observations: KLXL METAR (AWOS). Ceiling and visibility: NBM text guidance, hourly to 25 h and 3-hourly to about 72 h; NBM has no ceiling or visibility past that, so those days have no flight category and their outlook uses NBM sky cover (under 70% counted as likely VFR). Surface wind, gusts, temperature, sky cover and precipitation: NBM (via Open-Meteo). Cloud layers, freezing level, winds aloft and density altitude: GFS (via Open-Meteo). Crosswind uses the runway with the least crosswind. A supplement for planning only: always get an official briefing. Updated about every 30 minutes.
